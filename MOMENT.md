# The moment

1. **Person (demo):** Ms. Ana Rivera, 4th grade teacher, Harbor View Elementary (demo school),
   Oakland, California. 26 children. Families speak English, Spanish and Vietnamese.
2. **Moment:** Thursday, 3:10pm. The coach company's quote: 49-seat coach, $1,440, confirm and pay by
   Friday noon. The museum: $8 per child. The trip runs only with at least 18 children. 11 slips are back.
3. **Today:** she pays $1,440 on her own card, sends paper slips and a cash envelope, and chases
   families for weeks. Some never pay. Some children stay home because the family cannot pay $88 at once.
4. **Failure:** the teacher is out of pocket; the trip cancels late; families pay different amounts by
   hand; the office reconciles cash in a spreadsheet.
5. **Cost:**

   | PayPal closed Money Pools in 2021 | https://newsroom.uk.paypal-corp.com/evolving-the-ways-you-can-collect-and-raise-money |
6. **Action completed in the world:** the trip is confirmed, every family is charged its exact share,
   holds above the share are released, and the coach company and the museum are paid by PayPal Payouts.
7. **What only people can do:** the teacher confirms the quote and the close (the school's signature);
   each parent approves their own hold on PayPal (their wallet signs); the office records cash it
   physically received.
8. **Insider words:** the console is "The Register". Families get a "slip".
9. **Everyone assumes / in reality:** everyone assumes a group payment needs one payer up front; in
   reality PayPal can hold every family's share separately and charge each the real price later.
10. **Second domain on the same engine:** a youth football team's tournament bus, as a template switch.

## The numbers of the demo trip

- At 26 children: total $1,648.00, share $63.39 or $63.38 (remainder cents in join order).
